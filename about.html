About Winlark Mines

Reliable materials from our own quarry

Quality materials, reliable service

Winlark Mines is engaged in the trading and supply of construction materials, serving construction companies, contractors, builders, infrastructure projects and other businesses.

With our quarry and crusher operations, we have greater control over sourcing and processing. That helps us keep quality consistent and supply dependable. Our focus is simple: quality materials, reliable service and timely delivery.

From quarry to construction site

Quarry Operations

We source and process quality stone materials for various construction applications.

Crushing & Processing

Our crushers process raw materials into the sizes and specifications projects require.

Material Trading

We supply a wide range of construction materials to contractors, builders and infrastructure projects.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and recognized name in the construction materials industry by delivering quality products, reliable supply and sustainable growth.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality construction materials through efficient quarrying, crushing and trading operations, while maintaining customer satisfaction, operational excellence and long-term business relationships.
