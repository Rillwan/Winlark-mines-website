About Winlark Minerals

Reliable materials from quarry to site

Quality materials, reliable service

Winlark Minerals is engaged in the trading and supply of construction materials, serving construction companies, contractors, builders, infrastructure projects and other businesses.

We have our own quarry and associations with quarry and crusher units at multiple locations. For every requirement we choose the source that gives the best material at competitive rates, and arrange transport to your site. Our focus is simple: quality materials, fair pricing and dependable delivery.

Many crushers, one best choice
