About Winlark

Winlark Minerals supplies construction materials to contractors, builders, infrastructure projects and other businesses. We work from our own quarry and a network of associated crushers, choosing the best source for each requirement, so quality stays consistent and costs stay low.

Our Vision

To become a trusted, recognized name in construction materials through quality products, reliable supply and sustainable growth.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality materials at the best price through our own quarry and associated crushers, with dependable transport and long-term customer relationships.
