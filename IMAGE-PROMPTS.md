# Winlark Mines: image prompts

Style for all: photorealistic, natural daylight, warm earthy tones with a touch of golden light, sharp detail, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Save as JPG/WebP, under 250 KB each after compression, and keep the file names below.

## Product images (4:3, 1400x1050) replace the picsum links in index.html and products.html
1. **aggregates.jpg** Close-up of a heap of mixed construction aggregate of varied grey and tan crushed stone pieces, shallow depth of field, quarry yard in the soft-focus background.
2. **crushedstone.jpg** Freshly crushed angular grey granite stone chips in a large neat pile, a wheel loader blurred in the background, clear sky.
3. **msand.jpg** Fine manufactured sand in a smooth cone-shaped pile on a clean concrete yard, sunlight showing the grain texture.
4. **quarryproducts.jpg** Large rough-cut quarry stone blocks stacked at the base of a rock quarry face, dramatic side light.
5. **manufactured.jpg** Processed graded aggregates in three neat separate heaps of different sizes beside a crusher plant conveyor.
6. **otherbuild.jpg** Construction materials in a supply yard: stacked stone, gravel piles and a parked dump truck, wide shot.
